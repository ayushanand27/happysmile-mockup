HappySmile: AI first call for web enquiries

A fictional case mockup. Two clickable screens: the moment a person asks to be contacted, and what HappySmile sees afterwards.

Everything is clickable. Try "Call me" on screen 1, then open screen 2.

Design choices on this screen

Two buttons, two consents. Tapping "Call me" allows a call; "WhatsApp me" allows WhatsApp. Nothing else.

AI is named up front on the form and again at the start of the call. Never passes as human.

Promise is small and testable: a call within 2 minutes, 9am to 8pm. Outside hours, the first call happens next morning.

One missed call, then one WhatsApp, then stop.
